🌱 **FreshLoop – Every Meal Matters!**

What if we could predict food waste before it happens?

FreshLoop is an AI-powered food waste prevention platform that makes food management smarter, easier and more sustainable. By combining Machine Learning and intelligent recommendations, FreshLoop helps restaurants, college canteens, grocery stores and households reduce unnecessary food waste.

🤖 **Predict demand** before over-purchasing.

📦 **Track inventory** and get alerts before food expires.

🥗 **Discover smarter ways** to use available ingredients.

♻️ **Redistribute surplus food** by connecting food providers with people and organisations in need.

📊 **Make smarter decisions** through AI-powered insights and analytics.


🌍 **Less Waste. More Value. A Better Tomorrow.**

This will be our first project.
